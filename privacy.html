Privacy

Last updated 27 August 2026.

This site is a personal portfolio for Chantell Collins. It doesn't sell anything and doesn't run ads. Here's the plain-language version of what it tracks, what it collects, and where that information goes.

What's tracked on this page

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to count three specific actions: clicking the email address, clicking "Book 15-min intro," and clicking through to LinkedIn. Nothing else on the page is tracked. GA4 doesn't run until you accept the cookie banner. If you reject or ignore it, none of this loads and none of these events fire, the site works exactly the same either way.

These three events tell Chantell that a click happened, not who clicked. GA4 sets a cookie to distinguish repeat visitors from new ones and processes standard technical data (approximate location from IP address, device and browser type, referring page). Google's own privacy policy governs how Google handles that data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Booking a call

"Book 15-min intro" takes you to Google Calendar's own booking page. That form collects your first name, last name, email address, and company name directly, Google processes and stores it, this site never sees or holds that information. Google's privacy policy applies there too. Only the click through to that page is counted here, as one of the three events above.

Connecting on LinkedIn

The LinkedIn links on this page are plain external links. Nothing is collected on this site beyond the click itself being counted. Once you land on LinkedIn, LinkedIn's own privacy policy takes over.

Cookies and consent

A banner asks for consent before any tracking loads, using Google's Consent Mode. Accepting stores that choice in your browser so you're not asked again on your next visit. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which resets the banner.

Where data goes

Google Analytics and Google Calendar are both operated by Google, which is based in the United States. Data collected through them may be processed there under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the current legal basis for that transfer. That framework was upheld by an EU court in September 2025, but it isn't fully settled, an appeal is expected and its long-term durability has been questioned since. Worth knowing plainly rather than glossing over.

Your rights

If you're in the EU or EEA, you have rights under GDPR over data collected about you, including access, correction, and deletion. For anything collected directly by this site (which is limited to the click counts described above), or with questions about any of this, email chantell.collins@gmail.com. For data held by Google directly (booking form details, GA4 data), Google's own privacy tools and policy are the right place to start.

This page is written in plain language on purpose. It's not a substitute for legal advice, and if you need a formal legal opinion on GDPR compliance, a qualified lawyer is the right person to ask, not this page.
