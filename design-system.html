System rules

01Gold means an action is still available: A tab you're already viewing is bold ink, not gold.
02Text is flat color: Ink stays full-strength, with no opacity variants, so hierarchy comes from the type scale. Bold is for headings, card titles, the selected tab, the footer copyright line, and label colons (Rule 10). Italic is for quotations only.
03Cards carry the tint, not the sections: Section wrappers stay plain `#F8F4F2`; only cards (stat, proof, capability panel) use `#F0EAE6`.
04Pills carry a border, never a fill or a dot: Dots appear only as list bullets and carousel dots.
05Hover never resizes anything: Buttons keep their border in the base state.
06No sixth color: Everything draws from the five tokens.
07No dark mode.
08No off-scale spacing: Every margin, padding, and gap uses one of the five steps. Each visual block is one section with one symmetric padding pair.
09Keyboard focus always gets its own visible state: Every link, button, and tab has a 2px gold :focus-visible outline, never suppressed.
10Commas separate; a colon only introduces: No pipes or middots. Use a colon only to introduce or give a status, with the word and colon bold: Status: Applied. Never mid-sentence.
11Sentence case: Capitalize the first word and genuine proper nouns (Chantell, Vienna, IBM, PMI-CPMAI), in the hero lines, headers, and body copy. "AI & operational excellence" is correct; "AI & Operational Excellence" is not.
12Touch targets: every standalone link and button has a tap area of at least 44px; links inside sentences are exempt. Dots keep their 6px size and 8px spacing, so each has a 14px by 44px tap area and the arrows are the primary control.
